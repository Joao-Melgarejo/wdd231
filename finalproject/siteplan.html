Individual Project Site Plan

Site Name

The Master Plumbing

This name was selected because the business is known locally as Gasfitero Fernando and also as The Master. The name communicates skill, confidence, and professionalism while keeping a direct connection to the real plumbing service.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to provide clear and useful information about a local plumbing service. The site will help customers understand the main services offered, learn about Fernando's experience, view service details, and quickly request help through contact information or a service request form.

The website will also support the business by presenting a more professional online presence for customers who need plumbing repairs, installations, maintenance, or general home plumbing support.

Scenarios

  • What plumbing services does Fernando offer, and can he help with leaks, pipe repairs, or installations?
  • How can I quickly contact The Master Plumbing to request a service at my home?
  • Does the business have enough experience to handle urgent or common home plumbing problems?

Color Scheme

The selected colors are based on trust, cleanliness, water, and professional technical service.

Deep Navy Blue

Hex: #0F3D5E

Used for headings, header background, footer, and strong brand areas.

Water Blue

Hex: #1C9BD1

Used for buttons, links, service highlights, and visual accents.

Clean Background

Hex: #F4F8FB

Used for page background, soft sections, and card contrast.

Text Charcoal

Hex: #1F2933

Used for body text and readable content areas.

Typography

Roboto Slab

This font will be used for main headings, section titles, and strong brand statements. It gives the site a stable, reliable, and professional feel.

Inter

This font will be used for paragraphs, navigation links, buttons, forms, service cards, and general interface text because it is clean and easy to read.

Planned Website Pages

The final website will include three main pages:

  1. Home: business introduction, hero section, quick service highlights, and call to action.
  2. Services: dynamic service cards generated from a local JSON file.
  3. Contact: contact information, service request form, and form confirmation page.
